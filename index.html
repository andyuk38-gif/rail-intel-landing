Competency engine

  • Start from a ready-made framework or build a custom cycle in four steps — method, details, criteria, events.

  • Assess against structured criteria, attach files and photos, and keep saving even if the signal drops.

Workforce records

Live checks on licensing, medical and training.

Whether digital licence scanning, medical details or training records, each section is reported live to your dashboard and identifies any issues including non-compliance.

  • Track medicals, including upcoming renewals, with a live dashboard feed, backed by system notifications and automated emails.

Scanning licence

Reading licence number and dates…

Digital Scanning & Recognition Technology*
  • Upload a train driving licence and Rail Intel reads the images — populating number, expiry, categories, language and restrictions for you to review, eliminating manual input errors.
  • If the front or back of the licence is placed into the wrong field, the system detects this and places each side in the correct holder — eliminating upload errors.
  • Rail Intel automatically alerts you if a licence is due for renewal or has expired.

*Digital Scanning & Recognition technology supports UK train licences only.

Frequently asked questions

What is Rail Intel?
Rail Intel is competency management software for rail operators. It tracks assessment cycles, medicals, licences and workforce records so expired skills are flagged before anyone reaches the railway.
Who is Rail Intel for?
Driver managers, competence teams, safety departments and operations leaders who need a live system of record instead of spreadsheets for competence, medicals and licensing.
Does Rail Intel include add-on modules?
Yes. Optional CMS add-ons include QA Verifications, Task assignment, Safety Briefs, Trainee Driver, Driver Reports, Leave & Absence and Medication Checks. Rail Intel Investigations is a separate app for evidence-first case management.
