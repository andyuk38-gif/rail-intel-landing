Cab Passes

Record cab passes in standard driving cab pass layout. Digital passes show a QR code on the front in a panel the same size as the photo, stacked under it on the right; all holder and issue lines stay on the left.

ℹ
Who can change cab passes

Edit, Delete, Renew and Revoke follow role permissions. Administrators and managers typically manage passes for their reportees.

Pass colours
  • Green — Authorised to Assess
  • Yellow — PTS (Personal Track Safety) competent
  • Blue — Operational Rules competent
  • Red — Issued with endorsements; usually accompanied by a green cab pass holder
  • Black — Typically used for route learning

Frequently asked questions

How are cab passes verified?

Each pass includes QR code verification that anyone can scan to validate authority and status against the live record.

Are pass colour classifications supported?

Yes. Issue workflow captures colour classification and validity so the pass in the pocket matches the system of record.

Does someone need a Rail Intel login to check a pass?

No. Scanning the QR code opens the live pass in a browser with no sign-in, so gate staff or other checkers can validate authority without a Rail Intel account.

Is Digital Cab Passes included in core Rail Intel?

Yes. Issuing and managing digital cab passes is part of core Rail Intel. Showing pass status on verification reports requires the QA Verifications add-on.

What happens when a cab pass expires?

Expired passes stay on the employee record with status clear at a glance, and the QR remains available for audit. From the pass preview you can renew, revoke, edit or delete without leaving the Cab Passes tab.
